To use this module:

1. Log in with a user who belongs to the **Allows you to view the Delivery button from the sale and see the stock quantity** group.
2. Go to **Sales > Orders > Quotations** and create a new quotation.
3. Add a storable product and click **Search More...** in the product selector.
4. Verify that the **On Hand Quantity** and **Forecasted Quantity** of the products are visible.
5. Confirm the quotation.
6. Verify that the **Delivery** button is visible on the sales order.
7. Click the **Delivery** button and verify that you can access the related stock picking.
